=== 390 px · 3. An existing vesting position is refused, before the wallet is asked for anything

--- 04-existing-position-refused (04-existing-position-refused-390.jpg)
    otc.fam
    Local test chain
    Make a deal v2
    
    Sell tokens to one buyer. Your tokens are held by the deal contract until the buyer pays; each side pays a 0.5% fee at settlement, and nothing before.
    
    Delivery
    Immediate
    The buyer gets the tokens when they pay.
    Vesting
    The buyer gets a new grant that unlocks over time.
    Existing position
    A stream or a locked allocation you already hold.
    The position you would like to sell
    What this deal does
    
    Nothing: this kind of delivery is not supported.
    
    Existing positions are not supported
    
    otc.fam cannot sell an existing vesting position. A Sablier stream, an allocation locked at another contract, a grant that can be revoked, a screenshot or a link: the page cannot check any of them and the deal contract cannot hold them, so none is accepted as delivery. Nothing was asked of your wallet. To sell tokens you hold, choose immediate or vesting delivery: the deal contract then holds the tokens themselves.
    
    v2 deal contract on Local test chain (0.5% fee each side, vesting): 0x89eb5891179fb8c2dbd1bb2d7167306aab3a7cd3
    
    v1 deal contract (no fee): 0xd07554f63fc661351b4976fcc9bf5401f6c388bf
    [in the form: delivery-existing chosen; existing-position = https://app.sablier.com/stream/LL2-1-1234]

    WALLET: the page only asked which account and which network; it never asked to connect, approve or send.
